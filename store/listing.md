### Description (Apple and Google full description)

Bible Storying Kenya puts the Chronological Bible Storying (CBS) stories from biblestoryingkenya.com in your pocket, for storytellers, pastors, and listeners across East Africa.

Chronological Bible Storying tells the Bible as one connected story, in the order God gave it, from Creation through the life of Jesus and the early church. It is used for evangelism, discipleship, and church planting among oral learners, people who learn best by hearing and telling rather than reading.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• CBS Stories: the core storying set, from the creation of angels to the ascension of Jesus
• Sonship Stories and the Book of Acts
• Stories in English, Swahili, Maasai, and Borana
• A cloth-art picture for every story, to show while you tell it
• Audio recordings you can listen to and learn from
• Short videos introducing Chronological Bible Storying
• Printable story booklets

MADE FOR PLACES WITH LITTLE OR EXPENSIVE INTERNET
• Download stories, audio, and videos once, then use them with no connection at all
• Choose whether downloads may use mobile data
• Audio keeps playing with the screen locked
• New stories from the website arrive automatically, downloading only what changed

SIMPLE AND PRIVATE
• No account or sign-in
• No ads and no tracking
• The app collects no personal information

Bible Storying Kenya exists to see God's people and their leaders in East Africa understanding, being transformed by, and communicating the gospel of grace to one another and to the world.
