Privacy Policy

Bible Storying Kenya app · Last updated September 25, 2026

The Bible Storying Kenya app is published by Obed Works LLC on behalf of Bible Storying Kenya. This page explains what the app does and does not do with your information. The short version: the app does not collect any personal information.

What the app does not do

  • It has no accounts, sign-ins, or passwords.
  • It has no analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.
  • It does not sell or share information with anyone, because it does not collect any.

What stays on your phone

Your settings (such as appearance and whether to use mobile data), the stories, audio, and videos you download are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Internet connections the app makes

When you are online, the app downloads stories, pictures, audio, and videos from these places:

  • biblestoryingkenya.com, the ministry's website.
  • GitHub (github.com and github.io), which hosts the app's story updates and videos.

Like any website, these servers can see the basic technical details of a download request, such as your device's internet (IP) address. The app does not send them anything that identifies you, and neither Obed Works nor Bible Storying Kenya uses those details to identify or track you.

The Contact Us form

If you choose to use Contact Us, a web form opens where you can type your name, email address, and a message. Only what you type is sent, and only when you press Send. It goes by email to the Bible Storying Kenya team so they can reply to you, and it is not used for anything else. The form is protected from spam by Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks that the sender is a person.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes what it does with information, this page will be updated first and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Obed Works at servant@obedworks.com, or to the ministry through the Contact Us page on biblestoryingkenya.com.
